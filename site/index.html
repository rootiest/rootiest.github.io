How it works

One account, no passwords

Your RootiestID signs you in to every service with a passkey. Set it up once and you're in everywhere.

Open where it can be

Public services work with any RootiestID. Member services are unlocked by group, on request through the support desk.

Our servers, your data

No ads, no trackers, nothing sold. Services run on servers we manage, are watched around the clock and backed up encrypted off-site. How we handle data.
