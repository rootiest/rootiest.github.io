This policy explains what information Rootiest Solutions LLC (“Rootiest”, “we”, “us”) collects when you visit rootiest.com or use any service we run under the rootiest.com, rootiest.dev, rootiest.fyi, rootiest.link and rootiest.org domains (together, the “Services”), why we collect it, where it is kept and what you can do about it. We have tried to write it plainly. If anything is unclear, ask us.

The short version

  • We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking on any Service.
  • rootiest.com sets no cookies and loads nothing from other companies.
  • We collect what each service needs to work: your RootiestID account and the content you choose to store.
  • Your data lives on servers we run. Where a service has to involve another company, such as an AI model provider, email delivery or encrypted backup storage, we name it below.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be deleted, at any time.

Who we are

The Services are operated by Rootiest Solutions LLC, based in Massachusetts, USA. We decide how and why your information is processed, which makes us the “controller” of it under data protection laws such as the GDPR. You can reach us at privacy@rootiest.com or through the support desk.

When you visit rootiest.com

rootiest.com is a static website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no scripts, fonts or embeds from anyone else. The only image it loads from another address is the live status badge, which comes from our own status page.

Like any website, our server receives the technical details of each request (your IP address, browser user agent, the page requested and the time) in order to send you the page. We do not keep access logs for rootiest.com. The network providers that carry the traffic may process these details briefly to deliver it and protect against attacks.

Your RootiestID account

RootiestID (auth.rootiest.dev) is the single sign-on account for the Services. When you create one we store:

  • your username, display name and email address, and a profile picture if you add one;
  • the public half of each passkey you register (the private key never leaves your device or password manager);
  • the groups you belong to, which decide which member services you can use;
  • a security log of sign-ins and account changes, including the IP address, browser and device, and time of each event.

When you sign in to a service with RootiestID, that service receives your username, name, email address and group memberships, and creates its own account record for you. Some services also set a sign-in cookie on rootiest.dev so you are not asked to log in again on every page.

What each service stores

Each service keeps the content you put into it, plus the account details it receives from RootiestID. The notes below cover anything beyond that.

ServiceWhat it keeps, and who else is involved
Status page and Status for AndroidNothing about visitors. The page and the app only read public service status. Notification subscriptions in the app go through our own push service.
Support deskYour tickets and messages, linked to your RootiestID name and email. Email notifications about your tickets are sent through Resend, which handles the recipient address and message text.
Chat (Mattermost)Messages, files and your profile. If you use the mobile app, notifications are relayed through Mattermost’s push proxy and then Apple or Google. We send only a generic “new message” alert, never the message text.
Rootiest AI (LibreChat)Your conversations, uploaded files and the search index built from them. When you send a message, the conversation and any attached files are sent to the provider of the model you chose (OpenAI, Anthropic or Google), which processes it under its own API terms and privacy policy. Web search queries go to Google. Server logs may include request details and are kept for troubleshooting.
Notes, Joplin Sync, Obsidian LiveSyncYour notes and attachments. Joplin and LiveSync both offer end-to-end encryption; if you turn it on, we cannot read your notes.
Photos (Immich)Your photos and videos, including the metadata inside them, such as where a photo was taken. Face and object recognition runs on our own server, and nothing is sent to an outside AI service. The map view loads map tiles from Immich’s public tile service in your browser.
Files, Drive and share linksThe files you upload, and the share links you create. Anyone who has a share link can open it. Transfer logs, including IP addresses, are kept for security.
PagesPublished pages are public. Pages under a private path need a RootiestID sign-in.
Git (Gitea) and GistsYour repositories, issues, gists, SSH and signing keys, and profile. Public repositories and gists can be seen by anyone. Your email address appears on your profile unless you choose “keep email private” in your settings. Some public repositories are mirrored to GitHub. Git requests are logged (IP address, page requested, browser) to deal with abusive crawlers; the logs roll over automatically within days.
Short links (Kutt)The links you create. Every visit to a short link records the time, the referring site, the browser and operating system, and the country, worked out from the visitor’s IP address. The IP address itself is not stored.
Coding Stats (Wakapi)The activity your editor plugin sends: project and file names, languages, editor, operating system, machine name and times. It is kept until you delete it.
Password Vault (Vaultwarden)Your vault is encrypted on your device before it reaches us, and we cannot read it. We store your email address, the encrypted vault, your list of devices and sign-in events.
Push Notifications (ntfy)Messages on the topics you use, kept for up to 7 days. An account is required.
MollySocketThe connection details the Molly app gives it, so it can tell your phone when a Signal message is waiting. It cannot read your messages.
Bluesky PDSYour Bluesky account (email address and a hashed password) and your public repository of posts, likes, follows and profile. That repository is public by design: it is shared with the wider AT Protocol network, including Bluesky’s own services, which have their own privacy policies.

Cookies

rootiest.com uses none. The Services use only the cookies they need to work: sign-in sessions, security tokens and your display preferences, such as light or dark mode. We use no advertising or analytics cookies, so there is nothing to opt out of.

When you contact us

If you email us or message us on Discord, Bluesky, Mastodon, Lemmy or Matrix, we receive whatever you send and your account details on that platform. Those platforms are run by other organizations under their own privacy policies.

How we use information

  • To provide the Services you sign up for, and to keep them running, backed up and available.
  • To keep the Services secure: to spot and stop abuse, investigate problems and protect your account.
  • To communicate with you about your account, your support tickets and important changes.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent your information. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not use your content to train AI models.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, our legal bases are: providing the Services you asked for (contract), keeping them secure and working (legitimate interests), complying with the law (legal obligation) and, where we ask for it, your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Companies that help us run the Services

We keep this list short on purpose. Each provider only receives what it needs to do its job.

  • Server hosting: RackNerd and Contabo (virtual servers in the United States), plus servers we own and operate in the United States.
  • Private networking: Tailscale connects our servers over encrypted links. It cannot read the traffic.
  • DNS and network: Cloudflare provides DNS for our domains and handles traffic for a small number of redirect-only addresses.
  • Backups: Backblaze B2 stores our backups. They are encrypted before they leave our servers, so Backblaze cannot read them.
  • Email: Proton handles our mailboxes and sends account emails (for example from Git and Short links). Resend sends support desk notifications. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards mail for rootiest.org.
  • AI models: OpenAI, Anthropic and Google, only when you use Rootiest AI with one of their models.
  • Mobile push: Mattermost’s push proxy, Apple and Google, only for Chat mobile notifications, which carry no message text.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone’s safety, or to protect the Services and their users from fraud or abuse. If Rootiest Solutions is ever reorganized or transferred, this policy will continue to apply to the information you gave us.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored and processed in the United States. If you use the Services from elsewhere, your information is transferred to the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country.

How long we keep it

  • Account and content: for as long as your account exists, or until you delete the content. When you ask us to close your account, we delete your data from the live Services within 30 days.
  • Backups: backups are taken several times a day and older ones are removed automatically. Deleted data can remain in an encrypted backup for up to about six months.
  • Security logs: RootiestID’s sign-in log is kept for about 90 days. Git request logs roll over within days.
  • Push messages: up to 7 days.
  • Support tickets: for as long as they are useful for helping you, or until you ask us to delete them.

How we protect it

All Services use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in uses passkeys. Administrative access is limited to a private network and hardware security keys. Backups are encrypted, and the Services are monitored around the clock. No system is perfectly secure, so we can’t promise that a breach will never happen. If one affects your information, we will tell you promptly, as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your information or close your account;
  • export your data in a usable format. Most services also let you export it yourself.

To make a request, open a ticket on the support desk or email privacy@rootiest.com. We may need to confirm that the account is yours. We will answer within 30 days and will not treat you differently for asking.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights. In the EEA and UK, for example, you can object to processing or restrict it, and complain to your local data protection authority. Some US states give similar rights. We do not sell or “share” personal information for targeted advertising, as those terms are used in US state privacy laws.

Children

The Services are not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes, we will also give notice on the status page or by email to account holders before the change takes effect.
