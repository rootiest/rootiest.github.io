Getting started

  1. Create your RootiestID. Sign up at auth.rootiest.dev and add a passkey. It works with your phone, a security key or your password manager.
  2. Open a public service. Anything marked Public works right away: choose Sign in with RootiestID.
  3. Ask for member services. Services marked Members are unlocked by group membership. Open a support ticket saying which ones you need.
